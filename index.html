How to play

  1. Draw for the other person. On your turn, tap the big button for a random route, or pick one on the map.
  2. Send the card. Copy the two questions into your chat. They answer one, or both if they're feeling brave.
  3. They tap what they did. One answer lays 1 car, safe. Both answers go to you for a rating: 😐 0 cars, 🙂 2, 🔥 3.
  4. Finish the line. A route counts once all its cars are laid. When complete routes connect Amsterdam to Warsaw, whoever laid the most cars picks the first date.
