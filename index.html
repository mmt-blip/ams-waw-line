How to play

  1. Draw a card. Whoever's turn it is draws a route with two questions. You both get the same card.
  2. Both write. Answer in the app. Neither answer is shown until both are locked in, so nobody has to go first.
  3. Showdown. Read each other's answers, then rate them: 😐 1 car, 🙂 2, 🔥 3. The rating you get is the cars you lay.
  4. Finish the line. When complete routes connect the two cities, whoever laid the most cars picks the first date.

  2. Send the card. Copy the two questions into your chat. They answer one, or both if they're feeling brave.
  3. They tap what they did. One answer lays 1 car, safe. Both answers go to you for a rating (whenever you have a moment, before you draw): 😐 1 car, 🙂 2, 🔥 3.
  4. Finish the line. A route counts once all its cars are laid. When complete routes connect the two cities, whoever laid the most cars picks the first date.
